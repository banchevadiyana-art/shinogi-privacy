Last updated: 6 November 2026

At SHINOGI, we believe that meaningful personalisation should never come at the expense of your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how SHINOGI ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use our mobile application, website and related services (collectively, the "Services").

We are committed to transparency, responsible data practices and giving you meaningful control over your information.

1. Who We Are

SHINOGI is a personalised motivation application designed to deliver content and experiences that resonate with each individual.

The data controller responsible for your personal information is:

  • Operator: Diyana Bancheva, an individual
  • Country: Bulgaria, European Union
  • Contact: hello@shinogi.app
  • Website: https://shinogi.app

2. Information We Collect

Account information. SHINOGI does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect an email, name, username, or password for an account, and there is no authentication provider.

Personalisation preferences. Work, direction, friction, why, project, and reminder time are stored on the device only. They are not uploaded.

Content you keep. Notes, one-line notes, and saved notes stay on the phone. They are not uploaded.

Technical and usage information. The Share anonymous usage switch in Settings controls optional anonymous usage. Sharing is off in effect unless a build includes a TelemetryDeck app id (EXPO_PUBLIC_TELEMETRYDECK_APP_ID). This source tree does not set that id, so nothing is sent. If a later build enables it, the provider is TelemetryDeck, the payload has no note text, why, or project name, and the switch turns it off.

Notifications. Notifications are scheduled on the device. SHINOGI does not receive or store a push token on its own server.

Support communications. If you write to hello@shinogi.app, we receive the message you send.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use what stays on your device to provide the app, personalise notes and small steps from your answers, remember those choices, and schedule reminders on the phone.

We do not share notes, the why line, or the project name with a third-party AI or analytics service.

If a later build enables optional anonymous usage through TelemetryDeck, and you leave the switch on, that limited information is used to understand which screens and actions people use. That sharing is not active in this source tree.

If you write to hello@shinogi.app, we use that message to respond.

4. Legal Bases

SHINOGI does not process your notes or answers on a server under a contract, because there is no account.

If optional anonymous usage is later switched on, the legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by turning the switch off.

When you write to hello@shinogi.app, we use that message to respond to you. Where the law requires us to keep or disclose information we actually hold, we meet that legal obligation.

5. Personalisation and Automated Processing

Your answers on this device may choose which notes and small steps you see. That choice happens on the phone.

We do not share notes, the why line, or the project name with a third-party AI or analytics service.

SHINOGI does not use automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for unrelated marketing.

There is no cloud storage of personal content, no authentication provider, and no SHINOGI server that stores a push token.

The only third party named in this policy is TelemetryDeck, and only as optional and currently not active. Nothing is sent unless a later build sets EXPO_PUBLIC_TELEMETRYDECK_APP_ID and the Settings switch is on. If that happens, the payload has no note text, why, or project name, and the switch turns it off.

If the law requires disclosure of information we actually hold, such as a message you sent to hello@shinogi.app, we may disclose that information.

7. Retention

Your notes, answers, and reminder time remain on the device until you use Delete all my data or delete the app. There is no server backup of your notes. There is no account-deletion flow, because there is no account.

8. Security

Personal content stays on your device. We do not operate a server that stores your notes. We take reasonable care appropriate to on-device storage. No method of storage is completely secure.

9. International transfers

Your notes are not transferred off the device. TelemetryDeck may process anonymous usage outside the European Economic Area only if that optional usage sharing is later switched on. It is not switched on in this source tree.

10. Rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to personal information we hold, ask for a copy where portability applies, withdraw consent, and challenge automated decision-making.

Erasure in the app is Delete all my data. There is no account-deletion flow because there is no account. For a request, contact hello@shinogi.app. If you are in the European Union, you may complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection.

11. Notifications and choices

Reminders are optional and are scheduled on the device. You can leave the reminder off in the app, or turn notifications off in your device settings. Disabling notifications does not block the core of the app. The note still waits in the app.

12. Children

SHINOGI is not intended for anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided information to us, contact hello@shinogi.app.

13. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we change the date beside "Last updated". Where a change requires extra notice, we will provide it.
